## BORING list (feel + depth <= 4), worst first

1. **Runner** (Presto), 1 + 2. A cyan square on a flat blue strip, one jump key, no theme, no juice. Idea: a toy rolling-ball or wind-up car that collects stars, with simple hill and ramp art, a trail and a screen shake on a crash.
2. **True or false** (Scout), 2 + 1. Two giant flat buttons and a line of text; the whole game is one tap per question. Idea: a toy "judge" robot or a gate that swings open green or red, a streak meter that lights bulbs, a short sound and a pop on each answer.
3. **Stack tower** (Presto), 2 + 2. Bare coloured bars on a navy box; no tower look, no camera climb. Idea: toy building blocks with wood grain and a tiny crane, the camera rises, a perfect drop makes a sparkle ring.
4. **Snake** (Presto), 2 + 2. Squares and a yellow dot on a dark field, no grid or walls drawn, no growth effect. Idea: a toy train (engine and wagons) collecting parcels, with track tiles, smoke puffs and a station goal per level.
5. **Brick breaker** (Presto), 2 + 2. Flat cyan rectangles, tiny 310 px board in a 1280 px window, ball and paddle plain. Idea: toy building bricks with 2 hit points and colours, a pop and tiny debris, bigger board, power-up capsules shown.
6. **Shooter** (Scout), 2 + 2. Four flat labelled boxes and a tiny cannon "!" shape; you press 1 to 4 and nothing flies. Idea: a toy rocket or cannon that actually fires a ball that pops the target; targets as toy balloons or tins, and a score streak.
7. **Memory match** (Scout), 2 + 2. Blue "?" tiles that flip to plain text; no flip animation seen, no art on the cards. Idea: toy cards with a shape back, a flip turn, matched pairs fly to a shelf, a timer ring.
8. **Sort into bins** (Scout), 2 + 2. Two long bars and a green bar of text; very short rounds (3 to 6 items) and **empty on 10 of 12 topics tried** (see problems). Idea: toy bins with lids, the item is a toy block that you toss; the lid claps when right.
9. **Series builder** (Scout), 2 + 2. Numbered slots 1 to 5 and green bars of words; all bars full width; no sense of "building". Idea: it is already being reimagined as Domino chain (G-13): keep that plan.
10. **Word builder** (Scout), 2 + 2. Letters shown as full-width green bars, long words push letters off the bottom (clipped "r"). Idea: wooden letter tiles in a row on a shelf (A B C blocks like its icon) and a tray that fills up.
11. **Ball sort** (Ember), 2 + 2 (level 1 only). Dark flat screen, three colours, tiny tubes in a 1280 px window, no pour animation seen. Idea: bigger glass tubes, balls that arc and clink, a level map with 3 stars.

Not boring but weak: Mahjong (flat 4x8 grid on Easy, 3 + 2 = 5), Merge (2048 clone with flowers, 3 + 2 = 5), Balloon pop (nice balloons but one question, one tap, 3 + 2 = 5).

## Real bugs found (for the owner to queue)

1. **Question text shifts too early** (Balloon pop, Shooter, True or false; choice kit): after an answer the headline already shows the NEXT question while the options, feedback and explanation belong to the previous one ("What is 10 x 7?" above "Count by 5s nine times to get 45"). Confusing and wrong for a learning game.
2. **Many cards open an empty game**: Sort into bins, Memory match, Series builder, Word builder, Fill the gap, Word search and crossword, Bridge builder have no content for most topics; the home card says "Topic 1 of 10" and opens the "has no questions yet" screen or, for Fill the gap, "0 of 0 right".
3. Enter does not start Door runner single player; Enter does not close the how-to dialog (Esc does) on Craft games.
4. Results screens of choice games ("Round finished, Nice work on ...") show no score, no stars, no key hints (Fill the gap and Stack tower show some).
5. Mind game-over dialogs show store-style "Remove ads" and rewarded "Continue" buttons on the web.
6. Wrong key hints on Merge (select/best) and Runner (no jump key shown).
7. "See my stars" link on the home page goes to `/trail.html`, which is 404 (see SCREEN-REVIEW).
