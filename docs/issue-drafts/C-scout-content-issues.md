# [change] game-content: Craft Scout starter content (7 issues, about 200 items each)

**Repo:** ya-spark/game-content. **Depends on:** issue 09 (the repo) and the owner reviewing `topics.yaml`. Posted one at a time.

## Size
About **100 items per subject and year**, 1,400 in total (maths and English, Foundation to Year 6). Packs of 20 to 50 items. Original items, `en-AU`, no mention of any curriculum, an explanation on every item, maths answers checked by the validator, English reviewed by the owner (a sample of each pack).

| Issue | Content |
|---|---|
| C1 | Maths, Foundation and Year 1 |
| C2 | English, Foundation and Year 1 |
| C3 | Maths, Years 2 and 3 |
| C4 | English, Years 2 and 3 |
| C5 | Maths, Years 4 and 5 |
| C6 | English, Years 4 and 5 |
| C7 | Maths and English, Year 6 |

## Scope of each issue
Write the packs as CSV, convert and validate them with the content tool, add the pool report to the issue comment, and mark the packs `live` only when the owner approves. Fraction answers use multiple choice (typed answers are numbers only). Foundation and Year 1 items favour pictures and short text so the younger level and reading aloud work.
