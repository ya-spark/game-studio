## Games
- **Sudoku:** 9 by 9, generated puzzles with a unique solution (a pure-Dart generator and solver, tested). Four levels: Easy, Medium, Hard, Expert (clue counts in the spec). - **Solitaire (Klondike):** draw one or draw three, undo, auto-complete when solvable, tap or drag to move, winnable deals where the generator can guarantee it (a setting). Free with ads.
- **Ball sort:** tubes of coloured balls, sort so each tube holds one colour, undo, add-a-tube once per level, levels generated from a seed with a solver proving each is solvable. Colours also carry a pattern for colour-blind players. Coin-locked at 300 coins.
