## Games
- **Mahjong solitaire:** match free pairs of tiles from layouts (at least 8 layouts, each proven solvable by construction), shuffle when stuck, hint, undo. - **Merge:** a grid where matching tiles combine into the next one (2048-style but with original look and a different theme, for example growing plants or gems, no copied names or art). Swipe moves, undo once per move, best score, a relaxed mode with no game over. Coin-locked at 300 coins.
