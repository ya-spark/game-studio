## Goal
Four fast arcade games for Mind Presto. Speed and score chasing; short rounds of 30 to 90 seconds; instant restart.

## Games
- **Snake:** swipe control on a grid, speed rises with length, wall wrap as a setting, one life, best score. Free with ads.
- **Brick breaker:** drag the paddle, ball physics in pure Dart (fixed-step, tested), power-ups (wide paddle, slow ball), 20 generated levels. Free with ads.
- **Stack tower:** tap to drop a sliding block; overhang is trimmed; the tower speeds up; perfect drops give a streak bonus. Coin-locked at 300 coins.
- **Runner:** one-tap jump over gaps and obstacles on a procedurally generated track (seeded, tested for fairness: no impossible gaps); score by distance; a rewarded Continue once per run. Coin-locked at 300 coins.
